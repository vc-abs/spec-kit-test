# Ganapati (Lord Ganesha)

Lord Ganapati, also known as Ganesha, is the elephant-headed Hindu deity revered as the remover of obstacles, patron of arts and sciences, and the lord of beginnings. His form is instantly recognizable with his elephant head featuring a curved trunk, large ears, and single tusk.

## Physical Attributes

Ganapati is depicted with:

- **Elephant head** with a gently curved trunk, large fan-like ears, and one tusk (ekadanta)
- **Four arms** holding symbolic objects: modaka (sweet), lotus, axe, and noose
- **Rounded belly** symbolizing abundance and contentment
- **Ornate jewelry** including elaborate crown, necklaces, armlets, and sacred thread
- **Rich traditional garments** in vibrant colors with gold embellishments
- **Mouse (Mushika)** as his divine vehicle, often depicted at his feet

## Symbolic Elements

Each aspect of Ganapati carries deep symbolism:

- Large ears represent the importance of listening
- Small eyes denote concentration and focus
- Curved trunk shows adaptability
- Large belly symbolizes generosity and acceptance
- Broken tusk represents sacrifice for knowledge
- Four arms holding symbols of spiritual and material well-being

## Visual Treatment

Ideal for highly ornate and detailed depictions with traditional Indian artistic elements including intricate jewelry patterns, elaborate crown designs, and rich color palette of reds, golds, oranges, and greens.
